How to Use

Optimized for short, controlled slides on a level surface (Y-Balance, reach tests, etc.).

  • 1 Place the iPhone screen-up and perfectly level on a low-friction sliding board or platform.
  • 2 Tap Enable Motion Sensors and allow access when prompted.
  • 3 Keep the device still and tap Calibrate. Wait for the “Ready” status.
  • 4 Tap Start, then smoothly push the platform. Distance updates live.
  • 5 When finished, note the Peak distance, then tap Reset for the next trial.

Note: Inertial tracking has inherent drift. Best results on very short distances (< 1 m) with a smooth surface and steady push. Treat readings as comparative rather than absolute laboratory-grade.
